## Things that affect the method

- **No quarterly levels.** The file has 12-month totals (`Total_Trans_Amt`, `Total_Trans_Ct`) and Q4/Q1 ratios. Q1 and Q4 have to be derived. See `ASSUMPTIONS.md`.
- **Everyone transacts.** The smallest 12-month transaction count is 10. No customer is dormant for the whole year.
- **Zero Q4 activity is rare.** 7 customers have a Q4/Q1 count ratio of zero, meaning no Q4 transactions. 7 of those 7 are attrited customers. The active rate therefore barely moves between quarters.
- **Inconsistent rows.** 5 customers have an amount ratio of zero and 7 have a count ratio of zero. 2 customers have a zero count ratio with a positive amount ratio, which cannot both be true. Rule used: no transactions means no spend, so Q4 spend is set to zero for them.
- **Ratios above 1.** 9.8% of customers spent more in Q4 than in Q1 and 6.6% transacted more often. Most customers declined.
- **Tenure spike.** 2,463 customers (24.3%) have exactly 36 months on book. That is far more than the neighbouring values and looks like imputation by the data provider. It inflates the 25-36 tenure band.
- **Top-coded credit limit.** 508 customers sit at the maximum credit limit of 34,516. Credit limit is not used in the decomposition.
