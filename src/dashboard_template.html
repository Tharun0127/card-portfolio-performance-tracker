How to read this

  • Identity. Spend = customers on book x active rate x transactions per active customer x average ticket. The four effects come from additive LMDI and add up exactly to the spend change.
  • Derived quarters. The dataset has 12-month totals and Q4/Q1 ratios only. Q1 and Q4 are derived per customer assuming a linear trend across the four quarters. Dollar levels are estimates. Two alternative assumptions move the portfolio change between and (scenario A) and leave the ranking of drivers and segments unchanged.
  • Scenario A keeps the same customers in both quarters. Scenario B removes customers flagged as attrited from the Q4 book.
  • Active means at least one transaction in the quarter. Every customer in this file transacts at least 10 times a year, so the active rate barely moves.
  • Limits. One Q1 and one Q4, so seasonality cannot be separated from trend. This is a public teaching dataset and says nothing about any real issuer.
